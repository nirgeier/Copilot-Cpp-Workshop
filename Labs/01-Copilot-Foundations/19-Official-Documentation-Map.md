## 6. Maintaining These Labs

When you update a lab:

1. Reopen the linked official article and note the access date in your workshop notes.
2. Change only the claim that moved. Do not rewrite the whole lab.
3. Keep exercises tied to files that exist in this repository.
4. Keep prose in ASCII hyphens, as required by `AGENTS.md`.
5. Rebuild the site and confirm the nav label is present.

```sh
mkdocs build --config-file mkdocs.yml
```
